Pop the Party brings together my love of colour, creative details and making people smile.

I’m a single mum based in Nelson, New Zealand, creating handmade cakesicles designed to make celebrations feel extra special. Between chasing after my toddler and late-night chocolate decorating sessions, Pop the Party became a creative passion that brings together my love for colour, detail, and making people smile.

Every cakesicle is handmade in small batches with care and creativity. I love creating custom designs to match different themes, personalities, and celebrations — whether it’s bright and playful, elegant and simple, or completely unique.

At Pop the Party, I believe desserts should not only taste delicious but also feel exciting, memorable, and personal. Every order is thoughtfully made, from baking and decorating to packaging and presentation.

Thank you so much for supporting my small business — every order, follow, share, and kind message truly means the world to me. ✨

I can’t wait to create something special for your next celebration!

Eve | maker, decorator & the person behind every pop.
